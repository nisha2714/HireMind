# Senior Backend Engineer

**Level:** Senior

## About the role
Own the design and scaling of high-traffic backend services and mentor the team.

## Responsibilities
- Design, version and secure REST/gRPC APIs
- Optimise database queries, indexing and caching
- Lead reliability work: observability, alerting, incident response
- Mentor engineers and review designs and code

## Requirements
- 5+ years building production backend systems
- Deep knowledge of SQL and NoSQL data stores
- Experience with microservices, messaging and distributed consistency
- Strong debugging of latency and scaling problems

## Nice to have
- Experience with Kafka or RabbitMQ
- Cloud experience on AWS, GCP or Azure
