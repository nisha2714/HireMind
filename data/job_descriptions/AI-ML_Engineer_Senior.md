# AI/ML Engineer (Senior)

**Level:** Senior

## About the role
Take machine-learning and LLM systems from notebook to reliable production services.

## Responsibilities
- Build and deploy ML and LLM pipelines to production
- Evaluate models and monitor drift, quality and cost
- Choose between fine-tuning, RAG and prompt engineering
- Partner with product on realistic expectations and limits

## Requirements
- 5+ years in applied ML
- Strong Python with PyTorch or TensorFlow
- Experience with MLOps: versioning, CI/CD, monitoring
- Experience evaluating LLM-based systems

## Nice to have
- Experience with vector databases
- Cost and latency optimisation for inference
