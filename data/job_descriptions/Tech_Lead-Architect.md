# Tech Lead / Architect

**Level:** Senior

## About the role
Set technical direction for several teams and drive large-scale system design.

## Responsibilities
- Design systems for high availability and scale
- Define architecture standards and review major designs
- Plan migrations (for example monolith to services) with no downtime
- Coach engineers and communicate trade-offs to executives

## Requirements
- 8+ years in software engineering, 2+ in a lead or architect role
- Deep experience with distributed systems and data architecture
- Strong observability and reliability practices
- Proven ability to align stakeholders

## Nice to have
- Experience managing technical debt across teams
- Cloud cost management
